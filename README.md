# 🛡️ ScamShield AI – Intelligent Scam Awareness Chatbot

## 1. Project Overview

ScamShield AI is an intelligent chatbot developed using **Google Dialogflow ES** to promote digital safety, cybersecurity awareness, and protection against online scams. The main purpose of this project is to help users understand common online fraud techniques, identify suspicious messages, and learn basic safety measures they should follow when using digital platforms.

With the increasing use of online banking, digital payments, shopping applications, social media, and messaging platforms, the risk of cyber fraud has also increased. Many people receive suspicious messages, fake prize notifications, phishing links, fraudulent investment offers, and impersonation calls. ScamShield AI aims to make cybersecurity information easier to understand and accessible through a conversational chatbot.

The chatbot provides information about different scam categories and guides users towards safer online practices.

## 2. Problem Statement

Online scams have become a major concern for internet users. Scammers may use fake identities, attractive offers, urgent messages, and misleading links to convince people to share personal information or make payments.

Many users, especially those who are not familiar with cybersecurity, may find it difficult to recognise these warning signs. ScamShield AI addresses this problem by providing a simple conversational platform where users can ask questions about suspicious activities and receive relevant awareness information.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of ScamShield AI are:

- To spread awareness about common online scams and cyber fraud.
- To help users recognise suspicious messages and fraudulent activities.
- To provide basic cybersecurity guidance through a chatbot.
- To educate users about phishing, investment fraud, shopping scams, and prize scams.
- To guide users towards appropriate reporting channels.
- To encourage safe digital behaviour and responsible internet usage.
- To create an easy-to-use chatbot using Dialogflow ES.

## 5. Chatbot Features

ScamShield AI includes several features designed to provide cybersecurity awareness and assistance.

### 5.1 Scam Awareness

The chatbot provides information about different types of online scams, including:

- Phishing scams
- Investment scams
- Shopping scams
- Fake prize and lottery scams
- QR code scams
- Bank impersonation scams
- Remote access scams

### 5.2 Suspicious Message Assistance

Users can ask for help regarding suspicious SMS messages, emails, and other communications. The chatbot can guide users to look for common warning signs, such as suspicious links, requests for OTPs, urgent payment demands, and requests for confidential information.

This feature provides awareness guidance. A complete technical analysis of arbitrary pasted messages requires a suitably configured backend or webhook.

### 5.3 Account Security Guidance

The chatbot provides basic information about account safety, password protection, and actions users can consider if they suspect that their accounts have been compromised.

### 5.4 Reporting Assistance

Users can receive guidance about preserving evidence, contacting their bank through official channels, and reporting suspected cybercrime through appropriate reporting platforms.

### 5.5 Safety Recommendations

The chatbot encourages users to avoid sharing OTPs, passwords, banking information, and other confidential details with unknown people.

## 6. Dialogflow Implementation

The chatbot is developed using **Google Dialogflow ES**. The project contains approximately **38 intents and 30 custom entities**, based on the current project details.

Intents help the chatbot identify the purpose of a user's message. For example, when a user asks about a suspicious SMS, the chatbot can identify a relevant intent and provide an appropriate response.

Custom entities help identify important information within user messages, such as scam categories, communication channels, payment methods, risk levels, and reporting requirements.

Slot filling can be used to collect required information from users when additional details are needed. Fallback handling helps the chatbot respond when it cannot recognise a user's request.

## 7. Testing and Optimization

The chatbot should be tested using different realistic scenarios to evaluate its performance and response accuracy. Suggested testing scenarios include:

1. Asking about a suspicious bank message.
2. Reporting a possible phishing email.
3. Asking about a fake investment opportunity.
4. Seeking guidance after a suspected shopping scam.
5. Asking how to report cybercrime.

Testing helps identify incorrect intent matching, incomplete responses, and areas that require improvement. The chatbot should be tested with different ways of expressing the same request to check whether the correct intent is matched.

## 9. Limitations

ScamShield AI is an educational and awareness-based chatbot. Its responses should not be treated as official legal, financial, or cybersecurity advice.

A response based on predefined Dialogflow intents does not automatically mean that a message or link has undergone a complete technical security analysis. Dynamic message analysis and live URL reputation checks require an appropriately configured backend or external service.

Users should verify suspicious communications through official sources and contact their bank or relevant authorities when necessary. Users should never paste passwords, OTPs, account numbers, or other sensitive personal information into the chatbot.
