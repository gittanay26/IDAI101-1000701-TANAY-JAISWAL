# ScamShield AI

A Dialogflow ES chatbot designed to help users recognize online scams and learn safe next steps.

## Features
- Scam detection guidance
- Phishing and suspicious-link awareness
- OTP and UPI fraud prevention
- Fake job and refund scam awareness
- Password and account safety
- Reporting and recovery guidance

## Technology
- Dialogflow ES
- Custom intents and entities
- Slot filling and training phrases

## Testing
The chatbot is tested using Dialogflow's Try it now console with realistic scam-related messages. Testing results should be documented before final submission.

## Safety Notice
This chatbot provides general educational guidance. Never share your OTP, password, PIN, or banking credentials. Contact your bank through official channels if you suspect fraud.
