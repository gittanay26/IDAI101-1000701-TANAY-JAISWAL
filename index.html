KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Scams can look convincing.

Learn about common tactics used to pressure people into sharing information or sending money.

✉

Phishing messages

Emails or texts that imitate trusted organisations and push you to click a link.

Ask about phishing ↗
₹

Payment & UPI scams

Unexpected collect requests, fake payment confirmations, or pressure to transfer money.

◇

Fake prizes & offers

Messages claiming you won a reward but asking for a fee or personal details first.

Ask about prize scams ↗
▣

Impersonation

Someone pretending to be a bank, delivery service, support agent, or official.

Ask about impersonation ↗
↗

Investment scams

Unrealistic returns, urgency, and requests to invest through unverified platforms.

Ask about investment scams ↗

QR & remote-access scams

A SIMPLE SAFETY CHECK

Before you respond,
remember these four rules.

A moment of checking can help prevent a costly mistake.

01

Stop the pressure. Be careful when a message demands immediate action.

02

Verify independently. Contact the organisation using its official app or website.

03

Keep secrets private. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or recovery codes.

04

Do not rush payments. Check the recipient and payment request before approving.
